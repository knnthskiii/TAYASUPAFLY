NETLIFY DRAG-AND-DROP VERSION — NO PLAY BUTTON

1. Put your WAV song in:
   assets/music.wav

2. Drag the whole UNZIPPED GENGO_NETLIFY_AUTOPLAY folder into Netlify Drop.

3. The website attempts to play the music immediately.

IMPORTANT:
Modern browsers may block audible autoplay. There is NO visible play button.
If autoplay is blocked, the first click/tap/key press anywhere on the webpage
starts the soundtrack automatically.

The website itself never waits on the audio and never shows a loading overlay.
